I'm a Master's student in Machine Learning, Systems and Control at Lund University, with an
undergraduate background in Measurement and Control Technology from Harbin Institute of
Technology. My current research, supervised by Davide Tateo, focuses on safe reinforcement
learning for bimanual robotic manipulation — combining Lagrangian SAC with a Constrained
Markov Decision Process (CMDP) formulation on a dual KUKA iiwa arm setup in NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

Before this, I worked on an EDAN70 course project applying similar safe RL techniques to a
peg-in-a-hole assembly task, which resulted in an IEEE-format paper and presentation.

Beyond research, I have broad interests spanning comparative religion, Western esotericism,
classical Chinese poetry, and the philosophy of technology. I'm always happy to connect with
people who share similar interests, whether in robotics and machine learning or in these more
humanistic pursuits.
